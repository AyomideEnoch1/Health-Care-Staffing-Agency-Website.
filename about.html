About Divine Fingers
Healthcare Services Inc.

Excellence in healthcare staffing, clinical education, and compassionate patient care across Ontario and Canada.

Leadership & EXCELLENCE IN CARE

Divine Fingers Healthcare Services Inc. (Corporation ID: 1592082-5) is a premier healthcare staffing agency and nursing education provider based in Scarborough, Ontario. We bridge workforce gaps by delivering pre-screened, credentialed Registered Nurses (RNs), Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs), Personal Support Workers (PSWs), and dietary/support staff to hospitals, long-term care facilities, retirement communities, and home care clients 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
