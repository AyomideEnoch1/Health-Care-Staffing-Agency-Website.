OUR PURPOSE & VISION

OUR MISSION

To support healthcare organizations by delivering experienced, reliable, and compassionate nurses and Personal Support Workers who uphold excellence in care, professional competence, and ethical conduct.

OUR VISION

To be a leading healthcare staffing agency, delivering qualified and compassionate professionals who effectively bridge workforce gaps across the healthcare industry.

OUR CORE VALUES

Respect for People & Practice

We treat healthcare professionals, clients and patients with respect and dignity. We prioritize patients’ needs and care in every interaction to ensure safe and compassionate care.

Accountability at Every Level

We take responsibility for the professionals we provide, and we keep to our promises. We respond quickly, honestly and decisively when challenges arise.

Partnership Not Transactions

We work alongside our clients not just for them. We involve, listen, collaborate and adapt to support the evolving care/needs.

Quality Over Quantity

We prioritize competence, professionalism, reliability and trust in every nurse and Personal Support Worker we deploy.

DIVERSE & CERTIFIED STAFF

OUR TEAM

At Divine Fingers, our greatest asset is the dedicated team behind our mission. We bring together experienced healthcare professionals, support staff and certified Registered Nurses, Registered Practical Nurses, and Personal Support Workers/Health Care Aids from various cultural backgrounds, who share a common purpose: enhancing healthcare experiences for providers, patients, and communities.

Guided by expertise and driven by compassion, we collaborate closely with healthcare organizations to address complex challenges and deliver meaningful solutions. Our approach combines deep industry insight with a thorough understanding of the evolving needs of both patients and care providers. Every member of our team is committed to supporting healthcare organizations in achieving greater efficiency, improving care delivery, and creating lasting positive impacts. Together, we are helping shape a more connected, responsive, and patient-focused future for healthcare.
