2 Scope of Health Care Staffing Services

Divine Fingers Health Care Services Inc. operates as a specialized medical staffing, workforce recruitment, and community care nursing relief agency across Ontario and Canada. Our primary operational capabilities encompass:

  • Clinical Facility Relief: Dispatching credentialed Registered Nurses (RN), Registered Practical Nurses (RPN), and Personal Support Workers (PSW) to long-term care homes, hospitals, hospices, and specialized medical clinics.
  • Travelling Nursing Program: Providing licensed travel nurses and healthcare staff for rapid response and short/long-term regional coverage across Canadian provinces.
  • Community & Home Care: Providing dedicated in-home support, respite care, companion aid, and non-emergency personal support.
  • Emergency 2-Hour Surge Coverage: Providing expedited clinical staff dispatch to affiliated facilities facing urgent unexpected shift vacancies.
Non-Emergency Medical Notice: Divine Fingers is a healthcare staffing agency and community support provider. We do NOT provide 911 emergency medical dispatch services. If you, a resident, or a patient are experiencing an immediate life-threatening medical emergency, call 911 or attend the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
