Operational Terms & Health Care Staffing Agreement

Please review these terms before registering your facility account:

1. Clinical Staffing & Facility Engagements

Divine Fingers dispatches certified Registered Nurses (RN), Registered Practical Nurses (RPN), and Personal Support Workers (PSW) to hospitals, retirement residences, and healthcare facilities. Dispatched staff operate under the host facility's administrative on-site direction, clinical orientation, and workplace safety policies.

2. 2-Hour Rapid Emergency Surge Dispatch

Our on-call coordinators dispatch registered staff for urgent shift vacancies across Ontario. Facilities agree to adhere to advance cancellation windows specified in their facility staffing agreement to maintain continuity of care.

3. Professional Regulatory Standards

All nursing personnel maintain active registration in good standing with the College of Nurses of Ontario (CNO), current CPR/BLS certifications, and clean Vulnerable Sector Police Screening (VSS) records.

4. Non-Emergency 911 Notice

Divine Fingers is an employment and healthcare staffing agency. We do not provide 911 emergency medical services. In the event of an immediate life-threatening emergency, call 911 immediately.

5. Portal Credentials & Account Security

Authorized facility representatives agree to safeguard their portal credentials, prevent unauthorized access, and report any security concerns immediately to our dispatch team.

6. Ontario Governing Law

These terms are governed exclusively by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

Statutory Compliance: PIPEDA (Canada) • PHIPA (Ontario)

Privacy & Data Protection Statement

How we protect personal and corporate information across our platform:

1. Zero Data Monetization Guarantee

We never sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your personal details, corporate accounts, or clinician profiles to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

2. Strict PHIPA Health Information Protection

Our caregivers and coordinators operate in strict accordance with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA). No unauthorized resident or patient clinical charts are stored on public web servers.

3. Cryptographic Security Standards

All web communications are encrypted with TLS 1.3. User passwords are protected using salted 12-round bcrypt hashing, and database access is governed by strict role-based access control.

4. Designated Privacy Officer

For inquiries, contact our Privacy Office at 17-2 Dailing Gate, Scarborough, ON M1B 1Z8, or email info@divinefingershealthcareservice.ca.
