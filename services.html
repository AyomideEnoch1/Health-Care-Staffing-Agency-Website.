OUR COMMUNITY CARE SERVICES

🧼

A. Personal Care Support

We provide comprehensive, person-centered support to promote independence, dignity, and overall well-being. Our assistive daily living services include:

  • ✓ Personal hygiene support (bathing, showering, sponge baths)
  • ✓ Assistance with dressing and undressing
  • ✓ Grooming services (shaving, hair care, personal care)
  • ✓ Support with rehabilitation programs & therapies
  • ✓ Continence care and support
  • ✓ Medication supervision and reminders
  • ✓ Assistance with mobility, transfers, and safe movement
  • ✓ Support with the use of aids and assistive devices
  • ✓ Assistance with eating and drinking
  • ✓ Meal planning and food preparation

Tailored to meet unique needs in the comfort of home and community.

🩺

B. Travelling Nursing Program

Bringing expert nursing care directly to where it’s needed most.

High-quality, on-demand clinical care delivered in homes, retirement residences, long-term care facilities, and community settings across Canada:

  • ✓ On-site health assessments & clinical monitoring
  • ✓ Wound care & advanced dressing management
  • ✓ Medication administration & adherence support
  • ✓ Chronic disease monitoring & management
  • ✓ Post-discharge follow-up care to reduce readmissions
  • ✓ Rehabilitation support & coordinated care planning
  • ✓ Collaboration with physicians & care teams
  • ✓ Preventive care, health education, & wellness coaching

Clinically sound care designed around Canadian healthcare needs.

💛

C. Support & Companionship

We offer meaningful companionship and practical support to enhance quality of life, promote social engagement, and support overall well-being. Services include:

  • ✓ Transportation & accompaniment to medical appointments
  • ✓ Support with light physical exercise & walking
  • ✓ Assistance attending social outings & community events
  • ✓ Support with rehabilitation & therapeutic activities
  • ✓ Engagement in leisure activities (board games, cards)
  • ✓ Assistance with grocery shopping and running errands

Building trust, reducing social isolation, and supporting active lifestyles.

🌸

D. Respite Care Services

Flexible and reliable respite care services designed to support primary caregivers while ensuring continuity of high-quality care for loved ones:

  • ✓ Temporary relief for primary caregivers (rest & personal needs)
  • ✓ High-needs attendant care for intellectual/physical disabilities
  • ✓ Patient support for responsive or challenging behaviors
  • ✓ Social support to promote emotional well-being
  • ✓ Companionship & assistance with community outings

Emphasizing dignity, safety, and individualized caregiver relief.

🧹

E. Light Household Support

Assistance with light household tasks to help maintain a clean, safe, and comfortable living environment while supporting independence:

  • ✓ Kitchen cleaning and basic household tidying
  • ✓ Meal preparation and cooking support
  • ✓ General housekeeping (vacuuming, dusting, polishing)
  • ✓ Sweeping and light floor maintenance
  • ✓ Assistance with day-to-day shopping needs

Delivered with respect, reliability, and personal attention.

🧠

F. Alzheimer’s & Dementia Care

Compassionate, person-centered support for individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease and other forms of dementia, focusing on dignity and safety:

  • ✓ Relationship-based care tailored to individual needs
  • ✓ Patience, empathy, & compassionate interaction
  • ✓ Cognitive stimulation & meaningful engagement
  • ✓ Support in maintaining a familiar, structured environment
  • ✓ Assistance & guidance with daily routines (ADLs)

Enhancing comfort, reducing anxiety, and supporting independence.
