Welcome to DIVINE FINGERS HEALTHCARE SERVICES Inc.

Divine Fingers Healthcare Services Inc. (Corp ID: 1592082-5) is a trusted Canadian healthcare staffing agency and education provider headquartered in Scarborough, Ontario. Under the executive leadership of Livina Akadinwa (RN, BScN, MPH), Business Manager & Lead Educator, and Hezekiah Adeyemi (PhD), Operations Manager, we provide hospitals, long-term care facilities, retirement homes, and community healthcare partners with highly qualified Registered Nurses (RNs), Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs), Personal Support Workers (PSWs), and dedicated support staff 24/7.
